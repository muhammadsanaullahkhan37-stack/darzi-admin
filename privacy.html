Darzi Khata - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Darzi Khata ("the app") helps tailors and boutiques manage orders, measurements and payments. This policy explains what information the app stores and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Account: your email address and password (the password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we cannot see it).
  • Shop data you enter: shop name, app PIN and staff PIN, customer names and phone numbers, orders, measurements, payment records and notes.
  • Feedback: if you send feedback, we receive your message, email, shop name and app version.

How the information is used

  • To run the app and to back up your data so it can be restored on a new phone.
  • To read and reply to feedback and to show announcements inside the app.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored on your phone and backed up to Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Authentication), which is operated by Google. Each account can access only its own backup. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.

WhatsApp messages

When you tap a WhatsApp button, the app opens WhatsApp with a pre-written message for your customer. You choose whether to send it. The app does not send messages by itself.

Customer information

You are responsible for the customer details you enter. Please use them only for your business.

Deleting your data

To delete your account and backup, email us from your registered email address and we will delete it. You can also uninstall the app to remove the data stored on your phone.

Children

The app is intended for business use and not for children.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
